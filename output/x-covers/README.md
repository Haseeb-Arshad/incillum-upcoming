# Incillum cover explorations

The final Charlie directions are in `charlie/`. This folder also retains the
earlier photographic explorations for reference. Those source photographs were
generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the prompts below;
their lettering was subsequently set in HTML. The final Charlie directions use
the reference image and vector typography rather than those photographs.

## Photo-generation prompts

1. Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: original photograph for an Incillum X profile cover. Create a cinematic but natural editorial architectural photograph of an empty contemporary commercial office at night. One black desk lamp illuminates an unmarked sheet of quotation paper on a timber desk; a switched-off monitor and distant city windows recede into shadow. The lamp's warmth is the only colour. Real materials, quiet exacting composition, restrained exposure, documentary realism. Place the lamp and the piece of paper together toward the right half and within the central horizontal band; allow calm deep charcoal negative space on the left. Make this a single photographic image to be used as a banner ingredient, not a mockup. No people, no text, no logo, no typography, no watermark, no vivid blue or neon, no science fiction, no fake legible documents.

2. Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: original photograph for an Incillum X profile cover. A beautifully observed overhead view of a dark commercial work desk after hours: one real sheet of blank ivory paper with an understated tabular grid and a slim black pen, illuminated by a single off-frame desk lamp; the rest falls into natural darkness. The sheet is a believable work object, not a magazine collage. Only a small believable pool of warm lamplight. Tight, orderly composition, deep brown-black and neutral paper, editorial photography. Place the sheet in the right half, centered vertically, and leave the left half especially dark and uncluttered for a separate typeset overlay. No writing or legible numbers, no hands, no devices, no logos, no on-image text, no graphics, no watermark.

3. Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: original photograph for an Incillum X profile cover. Early morning in a quiet empty modern commercial office, a dark timber desk beside a large window, an unmarked single sheet of white paper on the desk, an empty black chair, and a distant city under a soft overcast sky. This is the same workplace after a night of work: believable, sober, calm and human, beautifully composed as an architectural editorial photograph. Muted stone greys, soft neutral daylight, no coloured cast, no orange sunrise. Keep visual interest on the right, within the central horizontal band, leaving a quiet softly lit area on the left for separate typography. No people, no legible text, no brand marks, no screenshots, no overlaid type, no watermark.

4. Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: original photograph for an Incillum X profile cover. An intimate close-up of one commercially used piece of paper held in the pool of a real desk lamp at night, viewed from an oblique high angle, with subtle tabular ruling, a dark desk surface and the edge of a black lamp visible. The rest falls into naturally deep black shadow; this is quiet human-scale photographic realism about work carried through the night, not a product mockup or a graphic treatment. Tiny amount of warm light only from the lamp. Compose the lamp and page on the right half within the central horizontal band, left half nearly empty and dark. No visible writing or numbers, no people, no logo, no titles, no watermark, no theatrical neon, no fantasy.
